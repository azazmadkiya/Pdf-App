About PDF App

PDF App is a powerful, lightweight, and completely offline Android utility designed to help you view, merge, split, compress, annotate, watermark, convert (PDF to Image & Image to PDF), and manage PDF documents directly on your device with absolute privacy.

Key Features

  • 100% Offline & Secure: All document processing happens locally on your device. No data is ever uploaded to external servers.
  • Advanced PDF Viewer & Zoom: Smooth page rendering with pinch-to-zoom and pan gestures.
  • Comprehensive Tools: Merge multiple PDFs, split documents, compress file sizes, add text/image watermarks, rotate pages, convert images to PDF and vice versa.
  • System Integration: Open PDF files directly from your file manager or email attachments with seamless "Open with" support.
